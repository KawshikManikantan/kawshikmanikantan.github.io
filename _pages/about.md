I work on video reasoning in Gemini as a Pre-Doctoral Researcher at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/), Bengaluru. Most recently I co-authored [MINERVA-Cultural](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10649) (CVPR 2026, Highlight), a benchmark for long-video reasoning across languages and cultures.

Before this, I interned with the AI Models Group at [AMD](https://www.amd.com/) (2024–25) and did my B.Tech + M.S. by Research at [IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/), advised by [Vineet Gandhi](https://faculty.iiit.ac.in/~vgandhi/) and [Makarand Tapaswi](https://makarandtapaswi.github.io/) at [CVIT](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/). My thesis reformulated coreference resolution as [Major Entity Identification](/MEI/) (EMNLP 2024) and introduced [IdentifyMe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07466), a long-context mention-resolution benchmark for LLMs (NAACL 2025, Oral).

**Research interests**

- Long-video and multimodal reasoning
- World models and embodied reasoning
- Memory and long-context understanding
- Evaluating foundation models across languages and cultures

The common thread, from characters in novels to scenes in hour-long videos: tracking what changes over time.

I am applying to PhD programs starting in 2027. If this overlaps with your group's work, do reach out; [email](mailto:skawshik11@gmail.com) is the best way.
