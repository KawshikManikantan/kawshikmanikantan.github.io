I'm a Pre-Doctoral Researcher at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/) (Languages Group, Bengaluru). I work on video reasoning in Gemini: agentic training methods for efficient deep reasoning over long videos, and post-training for video understanding across languages and cultures. Part of that work became [MINERVA-Cultural](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10649) (CVPR 2026, Highlight), a long-video reasoning benchmark spanning 18 locales. I also do white-box analysis of cross-lingual factual retrieval in Gemini.

Before DeepMind, I spent six months with the AI Models Group at [AMD](https://www.amd.com/), where I improved a continual model-editing pipeline and built a benchmark of recent Wikipedia edits to test how well edited models keep up with new facts. Earlier, as an intern with Star Sports, I built an operator-free player-tracking system that ran live during the Asia Cup 2022 broadcasts.

I did my B.Tech + M.S. by Research at [IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/) with [Vineet Gandhi](https://faculty.iiit.ac.in/~vgandhi/) and [Makarand Tapaswi](https://makarandtapaswi.github.io/) at [CVIT](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/). My thesis work was on tracking entities in long text: [Major Entity Identification](/MEI/) (EMNLP 2024) reformulates coreference around the entities that matter and transfers across domains where standard coreference models do not, and [IdentifyMe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07466) (NAACL 2025, Oral) measures how well LLMs resolve mentions in long narratives.

**Research interests**

- Long-video and multimodal reasoning
- World models and embodied reasoning
- Memory and long-context understanding
- Evaluating foundation models across languages and cultures

The common thread, from characters in novels to scenes in hour-long videos: tracking what changes over time.
