I am a Pre-Doctoral Researcher at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/) in Bengaluru, working on Gemini with the Languages team. Most of my current work is on long-video understanding, specifically training agentic models to search and reason over long videos efficiently, and post-training Gemini on videos across different languages and cultural contexts (including [MINERVA-Cultural](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10649), CVPR 2026 Highlight). I have also worked on white-box analysis of how Gemini retrieves factual knowledge across languages.

Before joining DeepMind, I completed my B.Tech and M.S. by Research at [IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/), advised by [Vineet Gandhi](https://faculty.iiit.ac.in/~vgandhi/) and [Makarand Tapaswi](https://makarandtapaswi.github.io/) at [CVIT](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/). I started out working on movie understanding and kept running into how poorly off-the-shelf coreference models track characters across full-length scripts and novels. That led to my thesis work, in collaboration with [Shubham Toshniwal](https://shtoshni.github.io/), on [Major Entity Identification](/MEI/) (EMNLP 2024) for tracking central entities across domains, and [IdentifyMe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07466) (NAACL 2025, Oral) for evaluating long-context mention resolution in LLMs.

During my degree, I also spent six months with the AI Models Group at [AMD](https://www.amd.com/) working on continual model editing and evaluating how edited LLMs hold up as facts change over time. Earlier, I interned at Star Sports, where I built an automated player-tracking system that was used live during the Asia Cup 2022 broadcasts.

**Research interests**

- Video understanding and multimodal reasoning
- World models and tracking state over long horizons
- Memory, continual learning, and long-context modeling
- Multilingual and multicultural evaluation
