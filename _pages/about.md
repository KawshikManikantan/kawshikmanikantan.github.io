I am a Pre-Doctoral Researcher at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/) in Bengaluru, working on Gemini with the Languages team. My work focuses on training agentic models for long-video reasoning and post-training Gemini across languages and cultures, including [MINERVA-Cultural](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10649) (CVPR 2026 Highlight). I also study cross-lingual factual retrieval in Gemini from a white-box perspective.

Before joining DeepMind, I completed my B.Tech and M.S. by Research at [IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/), advised by [Vineet Gandhi](https://faculty.iiit.ac.in/~vgandhi/) and [Makarand Tapaswi](https://makarandtapaswi.github.io/) at [CVIT](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/). Working with [Shubham Toshniwal](https://shtoshni.github.io/), I proposed [Major Entity Identification](/MEI/) (EMNLP 2024), a formulation for tracking central narrative entities that transfers across domains and scales to long documents with 20x greater efficiency than standard coreference. I also led [IdentifyMe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07466) (NAACL 2025, Oral), a long-context mention resolution benchmark that separates referential reasoning from structured-output errors in LLMs. During my degree, I spent six months with the AI Models Group at [AMD](https://www.amd.com/), working on continual model editing and benchmarking how edited LLMs adapt to new facts.

**Research interests**

- Video understanding and multimodal reasoning
- World models and tracking state over long horizons
- Memory, continual learning, and long-context modeling
- Multilingual and multicultural evaluation
