Hi, I'm Kawshik. I am a Pre-Doctoral Researcher in the Languages Group at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/), Bengaluru, where I work on video reasoning in Gemini: post-training for long-video understanding across languages and cultures, and agentic training methods for efficient deep reasoning over long videos. I co-authored [MINERVA-Cultural](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10649) (CVPR 2026, Highlight), a benchmark for cultural and multilingual long-video reasoning.

Before DeepMind, I was a Research Fellow Intern with the AI Group at [AMD](https://www.amd.com/) (2025), working on continual model editing. I completed a B.Tech + M.S. by Research (dual degree) in Computer Science at [IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/) (2019–2025), advised by [Vineet Gandhi](https://faculty.iiit.ac.in/~vgandhi/) and [Makarand Tapaswi](https://makarandtapaswi.github.io/) at [CVIT](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/). My thesis, _Coreference without Bells and Whistles_, reformulated coreference resolution as [Major Entity Identification](/MEI/) (EMNLP 2024) and introduced [IdentifyMe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07466), a long-context mention-resolution benchmark for LLMs (NAACL 2025, Oral).

**Research interests.** I am interested in how models should learn from video: how to represent what changes in a scene, keep it in memory, and reason over it across long horizons. This spans long-video and multimodal reasoning, world models and embodied reasoning, memory architectures for long-context understanding, and the evaluation of foundation models across languages and cultures. A thread running through my work, from tracking characters across novels to tracking state across hours of video, is understanding what persists and what changes over time.

**PhD applications.** I am applying to PhD programs starting in 2027. If these directions overlap with your group's, I would be glad to talk; the quickest way to reach me is by [email](mailto:skawshik11@gmail.com).
